## Measurement commitments

Every compression level must retain its evidence, source revision, applicability limits and raw comparison. Never equate retrieval frequency, a signed origin, a Bayesian reliability mean or a synthetic ablation score with a demonstrated live causal benefit. Reserve exploration slots to recover selection support; retain all eventual outcomes, including abandonment and escalation. Require cost and latency next to accuracy. Treat external vendor results as absent until their adapters have actually run with recorded datasets and configurations.
